Best Market For Islaa7:
Egypt:
The Best for "Repair Culture" & High Volume
If Islaa7’s core mission is sustainability and fixing (rather than just
convenience), Egypt is exactly that,
●​ Why: Egypt has a population of 110 million. Unlike the UAE, where
people often "replace" broken items, Egyptians almost always "repair"
them.
●​ The Opportunity: The "fixing" market is massive but mostly unorganized
(offline). A company that organizes this chaos with an app and offers
guarantees can grow exponentially.
●​ Growth Potential: Massive volume. The market is growing at 15%
annually, the fastest rate in the region.

Services of Islaa7:

1. Repair request service
●​ Users upload a photo and a description of a broken item
●​ The app connects them with the right technician (electrician, mechanic,
etc.)
2. Technician booking & assignment
●​ The system assigns skilled workers based on the problem
●​ Users can “book” a repair instead of throwing the item away
3. Home & item repair coverage
●​ Household appliances
●​ Furniture
●​ Clothing (e.g. torn garments)
●​ Small items (like utensils)
4. Rewards & incentives system
●​ Users earn points for repairing instead of discarding
●​ Points can be redeemed for discounts or vouchers
5. Sustainable waste management support
●​ Encourages the 5Rs (Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Repurpose, Recycle)
●​ Helps reduce waste and CO₂ emissions
7. Proper disposal (if not repairable)
●​ If an item cannot be fixed, it is disposed of correctly (not just thrown
away)
Business Model
Islaa7 operates on a commission-based revenue model. For every repair
completed through the platform, Islaa7 keeps 10% of the transaction value,
with the remaining 90% paid directly to the technician. Based on an estimated
average repair value of AED 100 which varies by product type and damage
severity this translates to AED 10 in revenue per completed job.

Operations
Islaa7 does not provide tools or transportation to technicians. Each technician
is expected to have their own equipment and a reliable way to reach
customers. This keeps Islaa7's costs low and ensures technicians are
self-sufficient from day one.
